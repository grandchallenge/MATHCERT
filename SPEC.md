# MATHCERT_SPEC.md

## Purpose

MATHCERT is the certification and trust-boundary pillar of the Grand Challenge mathematical platform. Its role is to check what can be checked, reject what cannot be supported, and record precise claim status.

MATHCERT is Lean-first but not Lean-only. It accepts formal proof assistants, exact rational computations, interval arithmetic certificates, SAT/SMT proof artifacts, proof-producing symbolic computation, and independently replayable ledgers.

## Motto

> MATHCERT assays the metal.

## Responsibilities

MATHCERT owns:

1. **Formal definitions** in Lean or equivalent systems.
2. **Formal theorem statements** even when proofs remain future work.
3. **Checked lemmas and reductions**.
4. **Exact certificate verifiers** for finite computations.
5. **Interval certificate replay** for analytic or geometric computations.
6. **SAT/SMT proof replay** where appropriate.
7. **Claim ledgers** linking informal claims to checkable artifacts.
8. **CI gates** preventing silent drift.
9. **Assumption and dependency ledgers**.
10. **Formalization blocker reports** for missing libraries or unformalized background.
11. **Programme reference validation** for stable knowledge graph provenance,
    without allowing classifications or discovery metadata to determine
    certification.

## Non-responsibilities

MATHCERT does not:

- discover problems;
- speculate;
- rewrite mathematical campaigns as if certification already exists;
- accept a computation without replay path;
- accept a theorem statement whose informal meaning is unclear;
- substitute citation for verification.

## Certification modalities

MATHCERT supports the following modalities:

```text
LEAN_FORMALIZATION
COQ_FORMALIZATION
ISABELLE_FORMALIZATION
AGDA_FORMALIZATION
HOL_LIGHT_FORMALIZATION
EXACT_RATIONAL_CERTIFICATE
INTERVAL_ARITHMETIC_CERTIFICATE
SAT_SMT_CERTIFICATE
COMPUTER_ALGEBRA_CERTIFICATE_WITH_REPLAY
SPECIALIST_AUDIT_PENDING
```

`SPECIALIST_AUDIT_PENDING` is not certification. It is a status marker.

## Certification gate

A result may be called **certified** only if it reaches at least one of:

- checked theorem in a proof assistant;
- checked lemma/reduction plus exact certificate replay;
- interval certificate replay with documented assumptions;
- proof-producing SAT/SMT replay;
- finite exhaustive search with independently checked certificate format.

A result may be called **formalization-ready** if it has precise definitions, theorem statements, dependency graph, and missing-library ledger, but this is not certification.

## Lean-first policy

Lean/mathlib is the default for finite combinatorics, algebra, number theory, and many elementary analytic structures because it offers an actively maintained library and a small trusted kernel. Other systems may be superior for specific legacy domains. The policy is not Lean maximalism; it is certificate minimalism.

## Relationship with MATHSOLVE

MATHSOLVE produces proof narratives and candidate lemmas. MATHCERT strips them to checkable claims. If the narrative cannot be decomposed into claims, it is not ready for certification.

## First domain

The first MATHCERT domain is Union-Closed Sets. Its initial purpose is modest: formalize definitions, state Frankl’s conjecture, check small lemmas, and prepare finite-family certificate verification. It does not aim to prove the conjecture.
