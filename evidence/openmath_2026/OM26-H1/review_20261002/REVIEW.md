## Independent mathematical argument and theorem review

For a nondegenerate triangle, a non-support affine linear function has a zero in its open interior exactly when its vertex values include strictly opposite signs. Necessity follows because an interior point is a strictly positive barycentric combination of the vertices, and a nonconstant affine function zero there cannot have all vertex values of one weak sign. Sufficiency follows from convexity and a slight perturbation of an opposite-sign edge interpolation into the interior, preserving opposite signs before interpolation. A non-support line cannot vanish on all vertices, or on two distinct vertices without coinciding with their supporting line. Thus this predicate treats vertex-only tangency as allowed and subdivision as excluded.

OM26-H1-THM-001: Both directions of the pinned proof are sound for finite sets of distinct Euclidean straight lines. If an additional intersection is inside a side, the incident distinct line is transverse to that side and one local ray enters the open triangle; a face cannot have such a vertex. Conversely, a line crossing the open triangle must exit at two boundary points. If neither exit lies inside a side, both must be vertices; the line then equals a support, contradicting its status as an additional line. Entry at a vertex consequently requires exit within the opposite side. Consecutive distinct vertices exclude either side-interior exit. An empty open triangle is a single bounded complement component, since it is convex and its three support lines form its boundary.

Parallel pairs do not support bounded triangles. Triple concurrency collapses the pair intersections and is rejected. Distinct pair intersections for three distinct lines imply noncollinearity: two of them determine one support line, so the third cannot lie on it unless all three intersections coincide. Multiple lines touching a vertex create one arrangement vertex, not additional side subdivisions; touches whose other vertex signs have opposite sign are interior crossings. Coincident supports are invalid input, not permitted degeneracies. The graph equivalence uses the producer's explicit definition: finite edges between consecutive DISTINCT vertices, with nondegenerate 3-cycles. It does not assert that arbitrary geometric triangles containing intermediate collinear graph vertices are 3-cycles.
